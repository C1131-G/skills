# Fundamentals and valuation

Use 10 years of consolidated data where available (Screener.in). Thresholds are starting points; compare against the sector, not across sectors.

## Business quality

| Metric | Healthy | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| ROCE (non-financials) | > 15% for most of 10 years | < 12% for 3 years |
| ROE | > 15% with D/E < 1 | High ROE driven only by debt |
| Revenue growth (10y / 5y CAGR) | > 10% | Flat or erratic |
| Profit growth vs revenue | Similar or faster | Profit up, revenue flat (one-offs) |
| Operating margin trend | Stable or rising | Falling for 3+ years |
| Cash conversion (CFO / net profit, 5y sum) | > 80% | < 50% — profits not turning into cash |
| Free cash flow | Positive most years | Negative while "growing" |
| Debt-to-equity | < 0.5 | > 1 (not banks/NBFCs) |
| Interest coverage | > 5 | < 3 |
| Working capital: debtor days | Stable | Rising sharply — possible channel stuffing |
| Dividend payout | Consistent | Paid while borrowing |

**Banks and NBFCs** use different metrics: ROA > 1.5%, GNPA / NNPA trend, CASA ratio, capital adequacy, credit cost.

## Accounting red flags

- Frequent auditor change or a qualified audit opinion.
- Large related-party transactions or loans to promoter group.
- Contingent liabilities large relative to net worth.
- Capitalised expenses rising faster than revenue.
- Other income a large share of profit.

## Valuation

| Method | Use when |
|---|---|
| P/E vs own 10-year median and sector | Steady earnings |
| P/B | Banks, NBFCs, asset-heavy cyclicals |
| EV/EBITDA | Comparing firms with different debt |
| Earnings yield vs 10-year G-sec yield | Sanity check: earnings yield far below bond yield needs high growth to justify |
| PEG (P/E ÷ growth) | Growth companies; < 1.5 is reasonable |
| Reverse DCF | Ask what growth the price already implies, and whether that is believable |
| Owner-earnings DCF | Final intrinsic value; use conservative growth and a 12% discount rate |

Write the intrinsic value as a range, then the price that gives a 25%+ margin of safety. Never present a single precise number.

## Market context

Check Nifty 50 P/E against its 10-year median. When the market is well above its median, favour staggered buying over lump sum, and raise the margin of safety demanded.
