# Interview

Ask in batches of up to four with the question tool, offering options plus "Other". Keep answers in a short profile and restate it before scoring.

## Batch 1 — situation (before anything else)

| Question | Options | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| What is this money for? | Wealth over 10+ years / Retirement / A goal in 3–5 years / Regular income | Sets horizon and the dividend-vs-growth tilt |
| When might you need it? | Under 3 years / 3–5 / 5–10 / 10+ | Money needed under 3 years does not belong in stocks |
| If the portfolio fell 30% in a year, you would… | Sell / Hold and worry / Hold calmly / Buy more | Real risk tolerance, not stated |
| Do you have 6 months of expenses saved and no high-interest debt? | Yes / Emergency fund missing / Have credit-card or personal-loan debt | If not, the first recommendation is to fix this, not buy stocks |

## Batch 2 — experience and constraints

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| How long have you invested in stocks? | New / 1–3 years / 3–10 / 10+ |
| What else do you hold? | Mutual funds / FD, PPF, EPF / Gold / Real estate (multi-select) |
| Your income-tax slab? | Old or new regime, and slab — affects dividend and gains tax |
| Sectors or companies to avoid? | None / Tobacco, alcohol, gambling / Other |

## Batch 3 — style

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| How many stocks can you follow quarterly? | 5–10 / 10–15 / 15–20 |
| Preference | Large, steady compounders / Mix with mid caps / Open to small caps |
| Do you want dividends? | Yes, regular income / Don't care |

## Asked later (step 5, after the holdings review)

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| How much do you want to invest now? | Free amount in ₹ |
| How will you deploy it? | Lump sum now / Split over 3–6 months / Monthly SIP of ₹X |
| May the review also sell existing holdings to fund better ones? | Yes / Only exits with losses / No sells |

## Stop conditions

- **Needs the money within 3 years** → recommend debt funds or FDs for that portion; equity only for the rest.
- **No emergency fund or high-interest debt** → say this first; recommendations are conditional on fixing it.
- **Would sell on a 30% fall** → large caps and index funds, small allocation to individual stocks.
