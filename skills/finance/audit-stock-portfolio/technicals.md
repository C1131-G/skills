# Technicals — entry timing only

Technicals never decide whether to own a business. They decide **how fast** to buy something that already passed fundamentals and valuation.

## What to check

| Indicator | Read |
|---|---|
| Price vs 200-day moving average | Above = long-term uptrend; far below = market doubts the story, check fundamentals again |
| 50-day vs 200-day | 50 above 200 = trend intact |
| RSI (14) | > 70 overheated — stagger buying; < 30 oversold — fine to buy if fundamentals hold |
| 52-week high / low | Near the high after a sharp run = wait or stagger; near the low = ask what the market knows |
| Volume on falls | Heavy volume on down days = distribution |
| Delivery % (NSE) | High delivery on up days = genuine buying, not intraday churn |
| Support zones | Prior consolidation levels for staggered buy prices |

## How it changes the plan

| Fundamentals | Technicals | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Pass, price below fair value | Healthy trend, RSI normal | Buy in 2–3 tranches |
| Pass, price below fair value | Overheated (RSI > 70, far above 200 DMA) | Buy a third now, rest on pullbacks |
| Pass, price below fair value | Falling hard on heavy volume | Re-check news and filings before any buy |
| Fail | Anything | Do not buy — no chart pattern overrides a bad business |

Never use stop-losses, targets, or chart patterns as a reason to trade a long-term holding in and out.
